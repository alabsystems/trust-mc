## Private solver component

AY: https://github.com/alabsystems/ay
The exact source revision and compiler-bound input bytes are recorded in
libexec/ay-origin.json. The source's pinned license/notice files are included
unchanged under libexec/ay-licenses/. The package's signed whole-tree root
authenticates the final relocated and signed helper bytes.
